# Finding things

## The command palette

`Ctrl+K` (`⌘K` on macOS) opens one box that searches your notes *and* runs
commands. It is the fastest route to anything, and it shows the keyboard
shortcut next to each command so you stop needing the palette for the ones you
use often.

## Search

The search pane in the left rail looks inside notes, not just at their names. A
second word narrows rather than widens, so `graph link` finds notes containing
both.

## The graph

Not a search tool exactly, but the fastest way to answer *what was near this*.
Nodes are notes, lines are links, and the note you have open is highlighted.
